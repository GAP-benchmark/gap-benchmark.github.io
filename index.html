TESTING PHILOSOPHY

The General AGI Probe Method

As tech companies announce AGI milestones, GAP was established to provide an unbiased, independent evaluation framework. We assess whether models demonstrate genuine generalization or merely recite pre-training memorization.

01

Unseen Algorithmic Reasoning

Dynamically generated tasks that cannot be found in internet datasets. Models must synthesize logical steps in real time.

02

Task Processing Throughput (TPTask)

Evaluates execution speed and planning coherence over multi-step autonomous tool calls and complex workflows.

03

Compute Cost per Task (CPTask)

Measures inference cost in dollars per completed task, establishing the realistic economic frontier for production use.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does GAP stand for?

GAP stands for General AGI Probe. As labs announce that they have achieved AGI, GAP independently probes and verifies those claims through standardized testing of real generalization capabilities.

What do the TPTask and CPTask metrics mean?

TPTask (Task Processing Throughput): Measures model processing speed and completion throughput across standardized benchmark problem sets.

CPTask (Compute Cost per Task): Measures the inference cost in USD required to complete each evaluation problem.

Why are only top 10 models displayed?

To keep the leaderboard high-signal and focused on current frontier performers, GAP displays the top 10 ranked models based on composite GAP Score.

How are missing attributes handled?

GAP's data ingestion handles incomplete records cleanly. If a model lacks an author or has unrecorded TPTask or CPTask values, it renders without errors or disruptions.
