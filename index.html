SCIENTIFIC RIGOR

The General AGI Probe Method

Traditional benchmarks (MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEval) suffered from pervasive pre-training contamination and dataset memorization. When a model scores high on static test banks, it often repeats memorized patterns rather than demonstrating general intelligence.

GAP tests genuine out-of-distribution reasoning, execution time per task in minutes (TPTask), and real compute cost in USD (CPTask) to establish an ungameable AGI frontier.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the General AGI Probe (GAP Benchmark)?

The General AGI Probe (widely known as the GAP Benchmark) is an open, empirical evaluation suite that independently probes frontier AI model capabilities. Rather than relying on static multiple-choice questions susceptible to web contamination, GAP evaluates novel multi-step reasoning, execution latency in minutes (TPTask), and real compute expense (CPTask).

What AGI metrics are used in the GAP Benchmark?

GAP defines three standardized AGI metrics for frontier AI systems:

  • GAP Composite Reasoning Score: Procedural algorithmic synthesis across unseen logic problems (0.0 to 3.0 scale).
  • TPTask (Time Per Task in minutes): Wall-clock execution latency taken by the model to reach verified task completion.
  • CPTask (Compute Cost Per Task in USD): Inference API and compute expenditure required to execute each standardized solution.
Why is GAP the premier general use AI benchmark and top level AGI benchmark?

Traditional benchmarks focus narrowly on rote memorization (MMLU) or narrow math tricks (GSM8K). As a general use AI benchmark and top level AGI benchmark, GAP probes broad generalization, cross-domain agentic decomposition, and real-world efficiency across leading open-weights and closed API frontier models.

What does the TPTask AI benchmark metric measure?

In the TPTask AI benchmark, TPTask tracks the exact wall-clock duration in minutes required for an AI model to deliberate, execute, and verify solutions. While standard benchmarks measure tokens per second, TPTask measures true problem-solving velocity for complex multi-step tasks.

Where can developers and researchers access the raw benchmark data?

The official dataset is published live on gap-benchmark.github.io and mirrored on GitHub at github.com/GAP-benchmark.
