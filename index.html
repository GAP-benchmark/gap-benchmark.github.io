SCIENTIFIC RIGOR

The General AGI Probe Method

Traditional benchmarks (MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEval) suffered from pervasive pre-training contamination and dataset memorization. When a model scores high on static test banks, it often repeats memorized patterns rather than demonstrating general intelligence.

GAP tests genuine out-of-distribution reasoning, multi-step autonomous planning, and real compute cost to establish an ungameable AGI frontier.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GAP and what does it probe?

GAP stands for General AGI Probe. As tech companies claim they have achieved AGI, GAP independently probes and verifies those claims through rigorous testing of novel reasoning adaptability, execution throughput, and real compute cost.

What do the TPTask and CPTask metrics represent?

TPTask (Task Processing Throughput): Quantifies execution speed, tokens processed per problem, and planning throughput.

CPTask (Compute Cost per Task): Measures inference cost in US Dollars required to execute standardized benchmark tasks.

Where can I view the official benchmark portal and prompt logs?

Full task-level telemetry, trace dumps, and detailed evaluation write-ups are published on the official platform at https://gap.sdj.com.np.

How does GAP handle missing values in model data?

GAP uses resilient null-safe parsing: if an evaluated model has no recorded author, missing throughput, or unpriced CPTask, it renders cleanly with fallback indicators without breaking comparative visual charts.
